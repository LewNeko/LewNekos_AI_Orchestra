# Knowledge Service Skill

## Overview 
`knowledge_service` serves as the retrieval facade for the entire system. iits core philosophy is to provide context and access to information without ever designating "canonical facts" within the service itself. 

## Core Functionality
- **Context Navigation**: Orchestrates the gathering of information (context) from various sources (fact history, source documents, reader results, audit references).
- **Fact History Querying**
